## Dependency notes

- **002 and 005 surface `ALREADY_IN_GAME` more often until 001 lands.** Both
  create a new lobby for a player who may still hold a stale invited seat in an
  older game; 001 is what removes those stale locks. The dependency is soft —
  002 and 005 are implementable and shippable before 001 — but expect the
  « Tu as déjà une partie en cours » toast to show up in manual testing until
  001 is done.
- **005 benefits from 004's availability display in the invite flow**: 004 makes
  a friend's online/in-game state trustworthy at connect time, which is exactly
  the signal 005's invite list wants to render. Soft dependency: 005 works
  without it, it just cannot grey out unavailable friends.
- **006 and 003 both add cards to the friend profile screen.** They are
  independent in data terms but overlap in layout — whichever lands second
  rebases on the other's layout rather than re-deciding the card order.
- **007 revisits the post-playtest fix from commit `308d00b`** (the global 1.2 s
  chip freeze). It requires a **real-table validation** — a physical game with
  the app — before its row may be set to DONE; a green test suite is explicitly
  not sufficient evidence, because the behaviour it changes was derived from a
  physical playtest.

- **017's step 6 is the only part that needs 016.** Everything else in 017
  (vector sources, regenerated icon set, README swap) is independent and
  shippable now; step 6 — the monochrome emblem fading in above the engraved
  wordmark — is explicitly marked skippable and must be skipped while 016 is
  still TODO. Order does not matter otherwise.
